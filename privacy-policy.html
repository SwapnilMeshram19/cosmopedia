Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Cosmopedia ("the App") is a space encyclopedia app. This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it's used.

No account required

The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use it.

Data stored on your device

The App saves the following locally on your device only (not on any server we control): quiz progress and scores, saved topics and articles, reminder settings, notification preferences, and your selected theme and language. This data stays on your device and is deleted if you uninstall the App.

Location

If you use the Sky tab's "Use my location" feature, the App requests your device's location to calculate visible planets and dark-sky times for your area. Location is used only for this calculation and is not transmitted to us or stored on any server.

Third-party content sources

The App fetches live public data from third-party APIs to display content, including: NASA (imagery, Astronomy Picture of the Day), Spaceflight News API (news articles), The Space Devs Launch Library (launch schedules), Wikipedia (on-this-day history), and wheretheiss.at (ISS position). These requests may be subject to the respective providers' own privacy practices.

Advertising

The App displays ads through Google AdMob and Google AdSense. These services may collect and use device identifiers, approximate location, and usage data to serve and measure ads, per Google's advertising policies. You can review Google's practices at policies.google.com/privacy. Ads are marked clearly within the App.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the Google Play Store listing contact details.
